## The tokens this round added

Colours, in `src/styles/index.css`. Each has a value in the dark theme and another in the light one.

| Token | What it is for | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|---|
| `well` | The bar at the foot of a dialog, a side rail | shade 15% | ink 3.5% |
| `well-2` | The track a thumb or a window slides in: segmented controls, the tool group, the overview strip | shade 25% | ink 6.5% |
| `dim` | Laid over the part of a strip that is out of view | shade 38% | ink 12% |
| `instrument` | The ground a graph is drawn on (the curve editor) — dark in any theme, like the viewer | shade 35% | `#1c1d1a` |

Corners. With Tailwind's own `xs` (2), `sm` (4), `md` (6), `lg` (8), `xl` (12) and `2xl` (16):

## How the dark theme was kept

The seventeen screens were photographed again and compared with the ones from before, pixel by pixel (a channel may differ by 2).

- **The nine dark screens** differ in two places only: the ruler's labels (a different grey, V-06) and, on the screen with a clip selected, the clip's header in the inspector (V-01). Nothing else moved: not a corner, not a wash.
- **The three at the smallest window** differ in the transport and the clip's header (V-01, V-02).
- **The five light screens** differ where they were meant to: the tab rows, the tool group, the overview, the ruler, the dialogs' footers.
- The corner each kind of thing is drawn with was read back from the running editor: a segmented control's track 9 px and thumb 6 px, an icon button 7 px, a clip 7 px.
- [`CONTROLS-2026-11-13.md`](CONTROLS-2026-11-13.md): 2,715 controls over these screens before, the same 2,715 after — the same thing, under the same name, in the same state.

## What is not done

- **Other wells.** The same wash is written at other strengths in particular panels and is still a patch of grey on paper: the Copilot's and Integrations' code blocks, the multicam list, the compare and auto-director strips, the mixer's meter slots, the viewer's angle bar, and the zone past the end of the timeline. They were not moved onto the tokens because that would change them in the dark theme, where they are right, and none was photographed in the light one.
- **The speed-ramp graph** has the curve editor's problem in the light theme — white grid lines and a lime curve on grey — and also has its own labels inside it, which a dark ground would make unreadable. It needs the graph and its labels drawn as one dark instrument, not only a ground.
- **A segmented control's thumb** is the darkest surface in the light theme, so the chosen tab reads as pressed in rather than raised. It is clear which is chosen; it is not how the dark theme says it.
- **Two corners are still numbers** (a 13 px logo tile, a 1.5 px sliver in the compare strip), and one size is relative on purpose (`text-[0.86em]`, code inside a sentence of any size). The three miniature drawings of motion presets are drawings.
- **The five greyed rows** of the clip menu (V-07), by decision.
- The light theme has now been looked at on these 17 screens and the 45 of the first round — still not on every state of every dialog.
